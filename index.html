What it does

A small wallet with one job: hold a QTC key safely and use it when you decide to.

Security, in plain words

No custody, no recovery desk, no background signing.

Keys

On your device only

The recovery phrase is encrypted with AES-GCM 256 and a key derived from your password with PBKDF2-SHA256 over 600,000 rounds. It never leaves the browser.

Approval

Nothing signs silently

A website request opens its own window with the details. If you do not confirm there, nothing is signed.

Network

One endpoint

Balances and broadcasts go through the public QTC explorer only. No analytics, no tracking, no ads.

Nobody can restore a lost recovery phrase, including us. Twelve words on paper, in two places, before you receive anything.

FAQ

The five questions that come up first.

Is this Bitcoin?

No. Qubitcoin is an independent chain derived from Bitcoin. It uses the same address format, but the coins are different. Never send BTC to a QTC address or the other way round.

What does a transaction cost?

The wallet calculates a network fee of 1000 satoshi per input and shows it before you confirm. That fee goes to miners, not to us.

Where do I get QTC?

From an exchange, a swap site or another person. The extension does not sell coins.

What happens if I forget my password?

You restore the wallet from the twelve-word recovery phrase and set a new password. The old encrypted vault is simply discarded.

Does a website ever see my seed?

No. Requests for the seed, the password or the backup file are refused outside the wallet itself.
